3. Start a fresh customer (bootstrap)

  1. Open /register (Request Access) on the live site.
  2. Submit the form with a new email, valid phone, and your details.
  3. Pass: Success message; row appears under Admin → Request Access as Pending.
  4. Sign in as Admin → Request Access → open the submission → Accept (sends invitation).
  5. Pass: Status becomes Approved; set-password email arrives at the customer inbox.
  6. Open the link from that email only (not an old/expired link) → set password → land on login.
  7. Sign in as the new customer.
  8. Pass: Dashboard current step = Complete onboarding payment; My Journey shows Request access completed.
Fail if: expired link, no onboarding case after password, or Accept without email delivery.

7. Known product behaviour (not bugs)

  • Most mid-journey steps are self-confirm (honour system), not verified against VPS/broker/licence APIs.
  • VPS step has no questionnaire — confirm button only.
  • Questionnaire form only appears when that step is not already completed.
  • Demo readiness is intentionally locked for 28 days unless Admin force-completes that step only.
  • Do not force-complete other steps casually during UAT — only demo_readiness is the planned exception.

8. If something fails mid-journey

  1. Note the step name, what the UI shows, and whether an email/webhook seems missing.
  2. For payment stuck after “paid” toast: Stripe webhook may not be reaching the app.
  3. For agreement stuck on awaiting: Dropbox Sign webhook/template misconfiguration.
  4. Fix the root cause, then resume — or start again with a new email customer.
